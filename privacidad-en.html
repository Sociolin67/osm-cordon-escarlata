What data we process and why

Only the data you voluntarily provide through the contact form (name, email address, telephone number if given, town, Lodge and message), solely in order to reply to your request for information.

Legal basis: your consent when submitting the form (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).
Retention: for as long as needed to deal with your request and thereafter for the periods required by law.
Recipients: the District officers responsible for replying; no data are passed on to third parties unless required by law.

Data processors

FormSubmit receives and forwards the messages sent through the form. GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts the website. Google Fonts serves the typefaces and therefore receives the visitor’s IP address. International transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by standard contractual clauses.

Statistics and cookies

We use GoatCounter, a statistics service that uses no cookies and does not identify visitors; it records only aggregated data (page viewed, referrer, browser type). This website sets no first- or third-party cookies for advertising or tracking. If you install the website on your phone, the browser keeps a copy of the pages so they can be read offline; you can delete it in your browser settings.

The official Mark Masons’ Hall videos are shown in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com): YouTube stores no information about the visitor until they choose to play the video; from then on Google’s privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability through the contact form. If you consider that your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
