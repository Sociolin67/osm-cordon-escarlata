Message sent. Thank you for writing to us: we will reply as soon as possible.

If you are a Master Mason and would like to learn about the Secret Monitor, or a Prince of the Secret Monitor interested in the Scarlet Cord, please write to us. Your message will reach the District Grand Recorder, who will reply and put you in touch with your nearest Conclave or Consistory.

Your details are sent through the FormSubmit service and are used only to deal with your enquiry; they are not passed to third parties or added to mailing lists.

Other ways to contact us

You may also write directly to the District Grand Recorder: districtrecorderspain@gmail.com.
Brethren of the Secret Monitor may join the District’s private Facebook group.
