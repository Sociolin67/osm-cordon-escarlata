Secret Monitor Scarlet Cord

Two Orders, one path

Both Orders are governed from Mark Masons’ Hall in London and work in Spain through their respective Districts: the District Grand Conclave of the Secret Monitor and the District Grand Senatus of the Scarlet Cord.

Brotherhood of David and Jonathan

Order of the Secret Monitor

Inspired by the friendship of David and Jonathan, the Order fosters loyalty, mutual trust and brotherly care. It is open to Master Masons of regular Lodges and meets in Conclaves.

Discover the Order

Six degrees

Order of the Scarlet Cord

Inspired by Rahab’s scarlet cord at Jericho, the Order explores episodes from the Old Testament through six degrees. It is open to Princes of the Secret Monitor and meets in Consistories.

Discover the Order

The path to membership

The Secret Monitor welcomes Master Masons; those who attain the rank of Prince may apply to join the Scarlet Cord.
